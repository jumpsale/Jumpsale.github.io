Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about JumpSale, including products, orders, delivery, payments, reviews and customer support.

1. What is JumpSale?

JumpSale is an online shopping platform where customers can browse products, add products to their cart and place orders online.

2. How can I place an order on JumpSale?

Select the product you want to purchase, click Add to Cart, open your cart and proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery details and place your order.

3. How can I check my order?

You can check your previous orders from the My Orders section. Your order details, products, total amount and available order information can be viewed there.

4. Can I view my complete order details?

Yes. Go to the My Orders section and click View Details for the order you want to check.

5. What payment method is available?

Currently, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for eligible orders. Other payment methods may be introduced in the future.

6. Where does JumpSale deliver?

JumpSale currently provides delivery in selected areas of Delhi. Delivery availability depends on the selected delivery area and PIN code provided during checkout.

7. How long will my order take to arrive?

JumpSale aims to deliver confirmed orders within 48 hours after the order is successfully placed, subject to product availability, delivery location and other applicable circumstances.

8. Will I receive the same product that I ordered?

Yes. JumpSale takes reasonable care to ensure that customers receive the same product they selected and ordered on the website. We check the order details before dispatch to help ensure the correct product is delivered.

9. Is there a return or refund facility?

Currently, JumpSale does not offer returns or refunds after an order has been successfully delivered. Customers are requested to carefully check the product details before placing an order.

10. Can I cancel my order?

Order cancellation may depend on the current status of the order. If you want to request cancellation, please contact JumpSale support as soon as possible.

11. Is there a delivery charge?

A delivery charge may apply to an order depending on the applicable delivery conditions. The applicable delivery charge will be included in the order total during checkout.

12. How can I check product reviews?

Product reviews are available on the respective product details page. Customers can view ratings, review text and uploaded review images where available.

13. Can I submit a product review?

Yes. Customers can submit a product review from the product details page by selecting a star rating and adding their review. An image may also be uploaded where the review image option is available.

14. What should I do if I have a problem with my order?

If you experience an issue with your order, delivery or product, please visit the Contact Us page and provide the relevant order information so that your query can be reviewed.

15. Is my personal information safe?

JumpSale aims to handle customer information responsibly. For more information about how information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.

16. Where can I find the latest information?

Product availability, prices, delivery conditions and other website information may change from time to time. Please check the relevant sections of JumpSale for the latest information.
